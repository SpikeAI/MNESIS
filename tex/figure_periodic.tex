\caption{Free replay of periodic motifs over long horizons. The motifs are made periodic by copying their trigger window (the first $D=41$ steps) onto their last $D$ steps, so that the chain of Spiking Motifs closes on itself with a period of $T-D=959$ steps. After a single trigger window, corrupted by balanced bit flips with probability $p_\mathrm{flip} \in [0, 1]$ ($14$ levels, one curve per level, coloured by $p_\mathrm{flip}$ as given by the colour bar), the network runs freely for $100$ periods (about $96\,\mathrm{s}$); each period is scored ($F_1$ score computed on the spikes of the $M=16$ motifs pooled together) against the frozen motif. Every curve reaches its stationary value at the latest in the second period and keeps it until the last one.}\label{fig:periodic}
\end{figure}
