  \caption{%
    Effect of some key parameters on working-memory performance ($F_1$ score over $10$ independent runs, each training a new network on new patterns drawn from its own random seed, a given seed sharing its patterns across all values of a parameter; bars show the mean over these runs, and whiskers span the $5\%$ to $95\%$ quantiles of a beta distribution fitted to them). %
    \emph{Top:}
    The $F_1$ score rises steeply with the maximum delay $D$ (left) and with the number of neurons $N$ (right): both increase the mean number $p_A N D$ of spikes in a context window, and thus decrease the probability that a motif contains a gap of $D$ silent steps, at which its replay stops. %
    \emph{Bottom:}
    It decreases slowly with the motif duration $T$ (left), as a longer motif is more likely to contain such a gap, and does not depend on the number of stored motifs $M$ over the scanned range, up to $M = 26$ (right).
  }\label{fig:scan}
