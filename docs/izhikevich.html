Keys

space
pause / play: a pause waits for the closest next spike and stops on it (press again to cancel); the buttons Pause and Play do each half
c
chain: the current spike and its 41 ms context window in colour; the next spike (blue) appears at the first press of d, or while playing
n / b
next / back, spike by spike (with the chain, the context window moves onto the next spike)
d
with the chain, paused: explains one prediction in 3 presses — (1) the next spike and the dashed box of the 41 ms before it, (2) the delays, which appear from the past to the predicted spike (a spike slides along each connection and they all arrive together; b then d replays it), (3) the context window moves onto it. While playing: show / hide the delay lines
s
scenario: the presentation step by step, one step per press (shift + S: the previous step); the caption says what is shown
w
shade the last 41 ms before the present (always shown with the chain and in zen mode)
k / l
slower / faster
r
reset the view (all neurons, the 3 s period, speed ×1) and the colours of the chain
f / z
fullscreen / zen mode (no interface; the only text is the width of the time window and the speed, or «paused»; z leaves it)
? or h
this help

Trackpad and mouse

two-finger scroll
horizontal: move in time (pauses) · vertical: move through the neurons
pinch
zoom the time window (ctrl + wheel)
option + pinch
zoom the neurons around the pointer (alt + wheel); a trackpad pinch has no direction

Touch screen

one finger
drag to move in time and through the neurons
two fingers
spread horizontally = zoom the time window · spread vertically = zoom the neurons · moving both = move the view
click anywhere to close
